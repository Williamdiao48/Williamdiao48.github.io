01 / Capture

From open tab
to permanent shelf.

Paste a link and Personal Library fetches, cleans, and stores it for offline reading. Dedicated parsers understand articles and multi-chapter serials—not just single pages.

05 / Your data

Local first.
Cloud when you want it.

The complete app works without an account or server. Your database and files live on your machine. Optional sync adds cross-device metadata and per-item file backup only when you choose it.
