  2. Trading bot

    Kalshi bot

    Matches live public data, mostly weather forecasts, to open Kalshi prediction markets. Its LightGBM models are judged on simulated bets placed before each market settles: 72% won across 2,447 markets.

    Python, LightGBM

  3. Search engine

    Flight Itinerary Creator

    Treats live flight segments as a graph and plans multi-leg trips with A* search, including connections airlines don’t sell as one route. Results can be re-ranked by price or time without another API call.

    C++, Python, FastAPI, JavaScript

  4. ML research

    Twitch emote prediction

    Predicts how Twitch chat will react to a 15-second clip from its video and audio alone, combining V-JEPA 2 and Whisper embeddings with cross-attention. Built with ACM AI at UCLA.

    Python, PyTorch
